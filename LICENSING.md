## Why MPL-2.0

MPL-2.0 is a file-level copyleft license. It allows the package to be used in
larger open-source or proprietary products while requiring distributed changes
to MPL-covered source files to remain available under MPL-2.0. It also includes
express contributor patent grants and preserves license notices.

This is a better fit for a reusable local CLI and Python library than the
repository's former customized Business Source License, which named a different
product and organization, restricted production use, and was not an open-source
license. It is less restrictive for adopters than GPL or AGPL while protecting
improvements to the covered files more strongly than MIT or Apache-2.0.
